\Cref{fig:learning} measures both with $\nseq=\learnSize$ Laplace-chain sequences at
$\corr=\learnRho$ and $\learnSeeds$ replicates per level, against the information
reference of \cref{sec:theory}: the Cram\'er--Rao bound $1/\sqrt{\nseq\,I_t}$ for the
correlation and, by the chain rule at the Laplace member of the shape family, for the
excess kurtosis. The reference is for an unbiased estimator in a one-parameter family and
the estimator is an eight-component mixture with its iteration count selected on
validation, so the comparison is a yardstick, read level by level. Up to
$t=\learnLevelTzeropfour$ every fit recovers the shape and the dispersion of
$\widehat\kfour$ across replicates is within the sampling error of the reference
($\audSdKurtTzeropfour$ against $\learnCrlbKurtTzeropfour$ at $t=\learnLevelTzeropfour$;
every level in \cref{app:audit}). At $t=\learnLevelTzeropseven$ the fits split,
$\audCollapsedTzeropseven$ of $\audCollapsedNTzeropseven$ collapsing to a Gaussian
innovation and the rest shrunk; at $t=1$, $\audCollapsedTone$ of $\audCollapsedNTone$
collapse, and the reference has crossed the full range of the coordinate at
$t=\learnKurtBoundCross$: the level at which the information runs out is the level at
which the estimator stops estimating. The correlation is recovered to $\learnRmseRhoTone$
at $t=1$ against a reference of $\learnCrlbRhoTone$ and is lost only past the crossover
$\tstar=\learnTstar$, $\learnRmseRhoTonepfour$ at $t=\learnLevelTonepfour$ and
$\learnRmseRhoTtwo$ at $t=2$, of which part is budget rather than information: with the
\textsc{em} ladder extended to $\learnBudgetLadderMax$ iterations on the same seeds and
data (\textsc{e06c}; hollow markers) the two fall to $\learnBudgetRmseRhoTonepfour$ and
$\learnBudgetRmseRhoTtwo$ while nothing below $t=1$ moves by more than
$\learnBudgetMaxAbsDiffRhoLow$; past the crossover \textsc{em}'s contraction slows with
the information it has. The same coordinates estimated by the method of moments---the
lag-one product for $\corr$ and the per-site fourth cumulant divided by $e^{-4t}$ for
$\kfour$, whose marginal statistics are unbiased by \cref{thm:hermite} up to the
$O(e^{4t}/\nseq)$ plug-in bias of a sample cumulant---are
$\audMomOverEmRhoMin$--$\audMomOverEmRhoMax$ times worse for $\corr$ and
$\audMomOverEmKurtMin$--$\audMomOverEmKurtMax$ times worse for $\kfour$ at the informative
levels, never collapse, and show the asymptotic growth where the estimate is already
useless (log-slopes $\audMomSlopeRho$ and $\audMomSlopeKurt$ on $t\ge1$ against $2$ and
$4$; \cref{app:audit}): exact inference buys the efficiency below the crossover, and at
high noise the two estimators share the rate, since the likelihood score of a degree-$m$
coordinate is itself a degree-$m$ Hermite statistic, of which the lag-one and per-site
statistics used here keep only a part. Below the crossover both errors
grow more slowly than the asymptotic rates (fitted log-slopes $\learnPreSlopeRho$ and
$\learnPreSlopeKurt$ on $[\learnPreLo,\learnPreHi]$ against $2$ and $4$), as
\cref{sec:theory} predicts; the asymptotic window lies beyond the reach of
$\nseq=\learnSize$ sequences at this correlation, because the shape collapses before
$\tstar$. The clean-data fit gives the optimiser's own floor, $\learnRmseRhoTzero$ and
$\learnRmseKurtTzero$.
