\Cref{fig:learning} measures both with $\nseq=\learnSize$ Laplace-chain sequences at
$\corr=\learnRho$ and $\learnSeeds$ replicates per level, against the information
reference read from the exact-score computation of \cref{sec:theory}: the Cram\'er--Rao
bound $1/\sqrt{\nseq\,I_t}$ for the correlation and, through the chain rule at the
Laplace member of the one-parameter shape family, for the excess kurtosis. The
reference is for an unbiased estimator in a one-parameter family; the estimator is an
eight-component mixture with its iteration count selected on validation, so the
comparison is a yardstick, not an efficiency theorem, and it has to be read level by
level. Up to $t=\learnLevelTzeropfour$ every fit recovers the shape (none of the
$\learnSeeds$ replicates collapses) and the dispersion of $\widehat\kfour$ across
replicates is $\audSdKurtTzeropzerofive$, $\audSdKurtTzeropone$,
$\audSdKurtTzeroptwo$, $\audSdKurtTzeropfour$ against a reference of
$\learnCrlbKurtTzeropzerofive$, $\learnCrlbKurtTzeropone$, $\learnCrlbKurtTzeroptwo$,
$\learnCrlbKurtTzeropfour$: within the sampling error of a sixteen-replicate standard
deviation. At $t=\learnLevelTzeropseven$ the fits split: $\audCollapsedTzeropseven$ of
$\audCollapsedNTzeropseven$ collapse to a Gaussian innovation ($\widehat\kfour\approx0$)
and the rest are shrunk, with dispersion $\audSdKurtGoodTzeropseven$ against a reference
of $\learnCrlbKurtTzeropseven$ and a bias of $\audBiasKurtTzeropseven$. At $t=1$,
$\audCollapsedTone$ of $\audCollapsedNTone$ collapse, and the reference bound has
crossed the full range of the coordinate ($3$) at $t=\learnKurtBoundCross$: the level at
which the information runs out is the level at which the estimator stops estimating.
The correlation is recovered to $\learnRmseRhoTone$ at $t=1$ with dispersion
$\audSdRhoTone$ against a reference of $\learnCrlbRhoTone$, and is lost only past the
crossover $\tstar=\learnTstar$: $\learnRmseRhoTonepfour$ at $t=\learnLevelTonepfour$
and $\learnRmseRhoTtwo$ at $t=2$. Whether the excess over the reference at those two
levels is information or budget the run cannot say: there the validation-selected
checkpoint is the last rung of the \textsc{em} ladder in $\learnAtCapHighMin$ of cells
or more, against at most $\learnAtCapLowMax$ at every informative level, and a control
with the ladder extended sixfold is registered (\textsc{e06c}). The same coordinates
estimated from the same data by the method of moments---the lag-one product for
$\corr$ and the per-site fourth cumulant divided by $e^{-4t}$ for $\kfour$, both
unbiased by \cref{thm:hermite}---are $\audMomOverEmRhoMin$--$\audMomOverEmRhoMax$
times worse for $\corr$ and $\audMomOverEmKurtMin$--$\audMomOverEmKurtMax$ times worse
for $\kfour$ at the informative levels, never collapse, and show the asymptotic growth
where the estimate is already useless: log-slopes of $\audMomSlopeRho$ and
$\audMomSlopeKurt$ on $t\ge1$ against the predicted $2$ and $4$ (\cref{app:audit}).
Exact inference is what buys the efficiency below the crossover; at high noise the two
estimators coincide, since the likelihood score of a degree-$m$ coordinate is itself the
degree-$m$ Hermite statistic. Below the crossover both errors grow more slowly than the
asymptotic rates, as \cref{sec:theory} predicts (fitted log-slopes $\learnPreSlopeRho$
and $\learnPreSlopeKurt$ on $[\learnPreLo,\learnPreHi]$ against $2$ and $4$); the
asymptotic window itself lies beyond the reach of $\nseq=\learnSize$ sequences at this
correlation, because the shape estimate collapses before $\tstar$. At $t=0.1$ the
transition law is recovered to Hellinger distance $\learnHellTzeropone$, and the
clean-data fit gives the optimiser's own floor, $\learnRmseRhoTzero$ and
$\learnRmseKurtTzero$.
