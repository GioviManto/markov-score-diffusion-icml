\section{The second-order floor across correlation}
\label{app:phase}
The frozen configuration fixes $\corr=0.85$. The price of second-order closure is a function
of $\corr$ as well as of the innovation law, and this appendix reports it over
$\corr\in\{0,0.3,0.6,0.85,0.95\}$ for five families on the twelve-level schedule, computed
from the exact score with no estimation involved.
The heavy-tailed member at $\corr=0$ is excluded from every statement below: the
quadrature sweep of \cref{app:numerics} places it outside the validated envelope of the
operating grid, with essentially all of its belief mass outside the domain, and a
recomputation at a wider domain is pending. The ordering by $\kfour$ is read off the
remaining cells.

\section{Capacity: the previous reading and the stopping-rule artefact}
\label{app:capacity}
An earlier study of this model compared innovation mixtures of $\ncomp\in\{1,2,4,8,16\}$
at $\nseq\in\{128,512\}$ over sixteen seeds and concluded that no capacity improved on a
single Gaussian innovation, attributing the estimator's margin over the networks to the
Markov factorisation and its covariance alone. Two facts overturn that reading. First,
\cref{prop:lmmse} bounds every Gaussian-innovation kernel by the LMMSE floor, which on
the Laplace chain is $\lmmseFloorLaplace$ pooled over the schedule, whereas the same
study's own headline estimator reported errors between two and nine times smaller.
Second, its sweep selected only the initialisation on validation and ran every capacity
to a fixed cap of $1200$ iterations unless a $10^{-9}$ likelihood tolerance stopped it
first; the tolerance fired in $31$ of $32$ single-Gaussian cells, at a median of $71$
iterations, close to the validation optimum that the headline protocol selects for these
sizes, and in none of the $96$ cells with $\ncomp\ge4$, which therefore reported
checkpoints an order of magnitude past their optimum. ``The single Gaussian is as good''
was ``the single Gaussian is the only arm that stopped in the right place''. The
capacity experiment of \cref{sec:experiments} selects the checkpoint identically for every
capacity on a per-seed validation bundle and reports the untouched confirmatory seeds.
