\section{Reference computations behind the body}
\label{app:audit}
Six deterministic or cheap computations support statements in the body; each is a
registered experiment (\textsc{e15}--\textsc{e21}) whose outputs are frozen like every
other.

\paragraph{The locality floor (\textsc{e15}).}
For the Laplace chain at $\corr\in\{0.5,0.85,0.95\}$ and every level of the schedule, the
best estimator of $a_i$ from the window $x_{i-r},\dots,x_{i+r}$ is its posterior mean
given the window, computed exactly by sum-product on the sub-chain with the exact
marginal law of the window's first site (propagated on the grid) as initial factor; its
relative score error against the full-chain score is a floor for every radius-$r$
denoiser. At $\corr=0.85$ the pooled floors are $\audLocFloorrzeroRhozeropeightfive$,
$\audLocFloorrtwoRhozeropeightfive$, $\audLocFloorrfourRhozeropeightfive$,
$\audLocFloorreightRhozeropeightfive$ and $\audLocFloorronesixRhozeropeightfive$ at
$r=0,2,4,8,16$; the Gaussian twin's windowed LMMSE is
$\audLocGaussrfourRhozeropeightfive$ at $r=4$ against its full value
$\audLocLmmseRhozeropeightfive$, so the non-Gaussian part of the score is what a wider
window carries. At $\corr=0.5$ the radius-$4$ floor is $\audLocFloorrfourRhozeropfive$.
For the Gaussian chain the score's dependence on a site at distance $k$ decays as
$r(\corr,t)^{k}$ with $r=(A-\sqrt{A^2-B^2})/B$, $A=\Dt(1+\corr^2)+e^{-2t}(1-\corr^2)$,
$B=2\Dt\corr$, from $0$ at $t=0$ to $\corr$ as $t\to\infty$.

\paragraph{The fourth-order correction (\textsc{e16}).}
With $y=\Sig_t^{-1}\vx$, $K=e^{-4t}\kfour$ and $A_{ml}=\sum_{jk}K_{mjkl}(\Sig_t^{-1})_{jk}$,
the first Edgeworth correction around the twin is
$\epsilon(\vx)=\tfrac1{24}\bigl[K[y,y,y,y]-6\,y^{\!\top}Ay+3\langle A,\Sig_t^{-1}\rangle\bigr]$
and the corrected score $\Sgauss+\nabla\epsilon/(1+\epsilon)$ with
$\nabla\epsilon=\tfrac16\Sig_t^{-1}K[\cdot,y,y,y]-\tfrac12\Sig_t^{-1}Ay$. Fraction of the
squared closure gap closed on the Laplace chain: $\audEdgeClosedLaplaceTzeroptwotwo$ at
$t=\audEdgeLevelTzeroptwotwo$, $\audEdgeClosedLaplaceTzeropthreetwo$ at
$t=\audEdgeLevelTzeropthreetwo$, $\audEdgeClosedLaplaceTzeropsixeight$ at
$t=\audEdgeLevelTzeropsixeight$, $\audEdgeClosedLaplaceTzeropnineeight$ at
$t=\audEdgeLevelTzeropnineeight$, $\audEdgeClosedLaplaceTtwopzeroseven$ at
$t=\audEdgeLevelTtwopzeroseven$; the bimodal and uniform chains cross ninety per cent at
$t=\audEdgeNinetyFromBimodal$ and $\audEdgeNinetyFromUniform$. Pooled over the
schedule the corrected score is worse than the twin, because the lowest levels dominate
the pooled energy and the expansion diverges there: the correction is a statement about
what the gap is made of at each level, not a competitor on the schedule.

\paragraph{Closed-form misspecification bias (\textsc{e17}).}
For a Gaussian truth with covariance $\Sig(\delta)$ the population limit of the
linear-Gaussian fit under the one-view protocol is
$\theta^\ast(\delta)=\arg\min_{(\corr,\ivar)}\sum_t\mathrm{KL}(\normal{0}{\Sig_t(\delta)}\,\|\,\normal{0}{\hat\Sig_t(\corr,\ivar)})$
and its pooled relative score error is
$b(\delta)^2=\sum_t\operatorname{tr}[(\hat\Sig_t^{-1}-\Sig_t^{-1})\Sig_t(\hat\Sig_t^{-1}-\Sig_t^{-1})]/\sum_t\operatorname{tr}\Sig_t^{-1}$.
Against the frozen EM--BP errors at $\nseq=2048$: the second-lag coefficient at the largest strength
$\audBiasArtwoEzeropfour$ (KL projection), $\audBiasLagoneArtwoEzeropfour$ (lag-one
plug-in) versus $\audBiasEmDArtwoEzeropfour$ measured; site-varying $\corr$ at its
largest strength $\audBiasSitevarEzeroponefour$ versus $\audBiasEmDSitevarEzeroponefour$;
long-range coupling $\audBiasLongrangeEzeropfour$, $\audBiasLagoneLongrangeEzeropfour$
versus $\audBiasEmDLongrangeEzeropfour$; rank-one latent at $\delta=1$
$\audBiasGlobalEone$ versus $\audBiasEmDGlobalEone$. The largest deviation of the
lag-one projection from the measured error over all cells is
$\audBiasMaxAbsDevLagoneD$. Small-$\delta$ laws: $b\approx\audBiasCoefArtwo\,\delta$
(second lag), $\audBiasCoefSitevar\,\delta$ (site-varying), $\audBiasCoefLongrange\,\delta$
(long-range, local exponent $\audBiasOrderLongrange$), $\audBiasCoefGlobal\,\delta^2$
(rank-one).

\paragraph{The closure constant and the heavy-tailed regime (\textsc{e18}).}
The per-sequence squared gap is a degree-six polynomial in nearly Gaussian variables and
heavy-tailed (maximum over $\audConstChains$ sequences
$\audConstMaxOverMedianLaplaceTfour$ times the median): the relative standard error of
the mean is $\audConstRelSeLaplaceTfour\%$ from $\audConstChains$ sequences, hence about
four times that from $512$. The constant is therefore quoted from $\audConstChains$
sequences with a bootstrap interval:
$\audConstRatioLaplaceTthree$ $[\audConstRatioLoLaplaceTthree,\audConstRatioHiLaplaceTthree]$ at
$t=3$ and $\audConstRatioLaplaceTfour$ $[\audConstRatioLoLaplaceTfour,\audConstRatioHiLaplaceTfour]$
at $t=4$ for Laplace. Across grids, with paired sequences, Student-$t_5$'s local slope
on the half-unit interval from $t=4$ is $\audWideSlopeStudentfiveNeightzerooneTfour$ at $(801,8)$,
$\audWideSlopeStudentfiveNonesixzerooneTfour$ at $(1601,16)$ and
$\audWideSlopeStudentfiveNthreetwozerooneTfour$ at $(3201,32)$, against $-8$: the
departure on the narrow domain is the truncated prior in the reference. Student-$t_7$ and
$t_9$ behave the same way. The uniform family departs identically on every grid from
$t\approx3.5$: that is the double-precision floor of the posterior means, reached by every
family near $t=5$.

\paragraph{Moment estimators (\textsc{e19}) and selection without the true score (\textsc{e20}).}
On \textsc{e06}'s data the moment estimator of $\corr$ has RMSE
$\audMomRmseRhoTzeropzerofive$, $\audMomRmseRhoTzeropfour$, $\audMomRmseRhoTone$ and
$\audMomRmseRhoTtwo$ at $t=0.05$, $\learnLevelTzeropfour$, $1$ and $2$; that of $\kfour$ has
RMSE $\audMomRmseKurtTzeropzerofive$, $\audMomRmseKurtTzeropfour$ and
$\audMomRmseKurtTone$ at $t=0.05$, $\learnLevelTzeropfour$ and $1$, with a bias of
$\audMomBiasKurtTzeropfour$ at $t=\learnLevelTzeropfour$. Selecting EM--BP's checkpoint by held-out
one-view log-evidence instead of by validation risk against the true kernel gives a test
error of $\audSelEvidenceA$ against $\audSelOracleA$ at $\nseq=32$, the size at which
selection matters most ($\audSelSameA$ of $\audSelNA$ seeds select the same rung; at
$\nseq=2048$ the validation curve is monotone through the ladder, so every rule picks its
top).

\paragraph{The information curve at $\corr=0.5$ and the scalar constants (\textsc{e21}).}
At $\corr=0.5$ the local slope of $\log(1/I^{\mathrm{eff}})$ on the levels
$[0.6,1.0]$ is $\audSlopeBetaWindowLo$--$\audSlopeBetaWindowHi$ for the shape coordinate
and $\audSlopeRhoWindowLo$--$\audSlopeRhoWindowHi$ for the correlation, against the
asymptotic $8$ and $4$: a sample-complexity experiment on those levels must be read
against the curve, not the asymptote. The Cram\'er--Rao reference for the excess
kurtosis at $t=1$ is $\audCrbKurtToneMtwozerofoureight$ at $\nseq=2048$ and
$\audCrbKurtToneMeightoneninetwo$ at $\nseq=8192$. In the scalar generalised-Gaussian
family an untruncated quadrature gives $I_t(\beta)e^{8t}$ over
$\tfrac1{24}(\partial_\beta\kfour)^2$ equal to $\audScalarConstbetatwoTtwo$ ($\beta=2$),
$\audScalarConstbetathreeTtwo$ ($\beta=3$) and $\audScalarConstbetaonepfiveTtwo$
($\beta=1.5$) at $t=2$, and the variance coordinate's $I_t e^{4t}$ equal to
$\audScalarConstgaussianvarianceTtwo$ times one half.
