\section{What explicit structure buys, and where it stops}
\label{sec:experiments}

All comparisons use the frozen configuration of \cref{app:protocol}: Laplace chain,
$\corr=0.85$, $\nsites=32$, twelve log-spaced levels on $[0.05,3]$, and the
schedule-pooled relative score error against the tree-exact reference (squared error and
reference energy summed over levels before the square root). Sixteen paired seeds are
the inferential unit; architectures and hyperparameters are selected on two development
seeds that never enter a reported number, and every arm's budget is selected on a
per-seed validation bundle disjoint from the test bundle.

\paragraph{The oracle ladder.}
Three reference points bracket every learned estimator (\cref{app:oracles}): the
isotropic score $-\vx$, which knows only the marginal variance, at pooled error
$\isoFloorLaplace$; the LMMSE oracle with the true covariance, at $\lmmseFloorLaplace$,
which by \cref{prop:lmmse} is the floor of every second-order estimator; and the exact
score at zero.

requires; the mixture kernel goes far below it. An earlier study of this model concluded
the opposite from a sweep whose single-Gaussian arm alone stopped at its validation
optimum while richer arms ran to a fixed cap; with the checkpoint selected identically
