\section{What explicit structure buys, and where it stops}
\label{sec:experiments}

All comparisons use the frozen configuration of \cref{app:protocol}: Laplace chain,
$\corr=0.85$, $\nsites=32$, twelve log-spaced levels on $[0.05,3]$, and the
schedule-pooled relative score error against the tree-exact reference (squared error and
reference energy summed over levels before the square root). Paired seeds are the
inferential unit---sixteen in the comparisons at the frozen configuration, eight in the
sweeps across regimes; architectures and hyperparameters are selected on two development
seeds that never enter a reported number, and every arm's budget is selected on a
per-seed validation bundle disjoint from the test bundle. Three information interfaces
are distinguished throughout: (A) the standard denoising recipe, clean sequences with a
fresh corruption at every step; (B) clean sequences with one fixed corruption each;
(C) one noised view per sequence and nothing else, which is what EM--BP sees.
Three reference points bracket every estimator (\cref{app:oracles}): the isotropic score
$-\vx$ at $\isoFloorLaplace$, the LMMSE oracle with the true covariance at
$\lmmseFloorLaplace$---the floor of every second-order estimator, by
\cref{prop:lmmse}---and the exact score at zero.
