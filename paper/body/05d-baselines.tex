\Cref{tab:baselines} compares six architectures trained under one recipe (interface~A)
with the configuration that won each family's screen on the development seeds
(\cref{app:protocol}). Weight sharing separates the fully connected network from the rest
at every size ($\baseWorstA$ against $\baseBestA$ at $\nseq=32$); locality is the better
prior when sequences are scarce, the \baseBestArchA{} being the best network at
$\nseq=32$, and stops paying once they are not: the window head moves only from
$\baseWindowC$ to $\baseWindowD$ between $\nseq=512$ and $2048$ while the global
architectures keep improving, the best reaching $\baseBestD$. That plateau is the
architecture's and not the radius's: the best estimator of any kind that sees only a
window of radius $r$ is $\E[a_i\mid x_{i-r},\dots,x_{i+r}]$, computable by sum-product on
the sub-chain, and at radius $4$ its pooled error is $\audLocFloorrfourRhozeropeightfive$
against the head's $\baseWindowD$ (\cref{app:audit}, \cref{fig:locality}). Given enough
clean sequences the networks pass the second-order floor, $\baseBelowFloorND$ of six
sitting below $\baseLmmseD$ at $\nseq=2048$ (\cref{prop:lmmse}); nothing intrinsic holds
them at second order. The structured estimator is ahead at every size nonetheless,
$\capEmeightD$ against $\baseBestD$ at $\nseq=2048$, a paired factor $\baseRatioBestD$
($[\baseRatioBestDlo,\baseRatioBestDhi]$, $\baseWinsBestD$ of $\baseSeeds$ seeds). That
factor is an upper bound on the advantage at this interface, since the largest networks
were still improving when their ladder ended ($\baseAtCapTransformerD\%$ of the
transformer cells at the last rung), and it is not information-matched: the networks see
clean sequences and a fresh corruption at every step, EM--BP one noised view per sequence
and no clean chain at all.

\paragraph{The comparison at matched information.}
Interface~C is EM--BP's own: one noised view per sequence split across the twelve levels,
no clean chain, and, since the true score is unavailable for selection there, the
checkpoint chosen on the implicit score-matching objective of a held-out noisy bundle.
Trained by implicit score matching on exactly that bundle, the \matchedBestArchD{} reaches
$\matchedBestD$ at $\nseq=2048$ against EM--BP's $\capEmeightD$, a paired factor
$\matchedRatioBestD$ ($[\matchedRatioBestDlo,\matchedRatioBestDhi]$, $\matchedWinsD$ of
$\baseSeeds$ seeds); the transformer reaches $\matchedIsmoneviewTransformerD$. Under
interface~B, the clean sequence supplied as a regression target but with one fixed
corruption per sequence, the same head reaches $\matchedFixedD$, a factor
$\matchedFixedRatioD$: even given strictly more than EM--BP sees, one corruption per
sequence is not enough. The factor that remains at equal information is what the
factorisation and the exact E-step are worth, and it is larger than the factor at the
standard interface, not smaller. EM--BP's own selection needs no true score either:
choosing its iteration count by held-out one-view log-evidence gives $\audSelEvidenceA$
against $\audSelOracleA$ with oracle validation at $\nseq=32$, where the rung matters
most, the two rules agreeing in $\audSelSameA$ of $\audSelNA$ seeds (\cref{app:audit}).

\paragraph{Length extrapolation.}
Fitted at $\nsites=32$ and evaluated against the exact score of longer chains
(\cref{tab:length}), the EM--BP kernel holds $\lenEmbpDLthreetwo$ at $\nsites=32$ and
$\lenEmbpDLtwofivesix$ at $256$: the law is local, so inference at any length is the same
recursion. The convolutional and recurrent networks improve slightly, as longer chains
have proportionally fewer boundary sites; the transformer with learned relative positions
degrades from $\lenTransformerDLthreetwo$ to $\lenTransformerDLtwofivesix$, a failure of
the positional encoding beyond its training length rather than of the score it learned
\citep{press2022alibi,kazemnejad2023positional}.
