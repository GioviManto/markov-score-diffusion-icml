  \caption{\textbf{Where the structure stops helping.} Ratio of the window head's error
  to EM--BP's against the misspecification strength, for four Gaussian departures from
  the chain with exact reference scores (rank-one global latent, long-range precision
  coupling, an AR(2) second coefficient at fixed lag-one correlation, site-dependent
  $\corr$), at two sample sizes. Above the dotted line the misspecified chain estimator
  still wins.}
  \label{fig:misspec}
\end{figure}
Every departure is Gaussian so that its score is exact, and every arm is scored against
that score. The ordering degrades smoothly in each mechanism and the strength at which
it breaks even is a property of the mechanism, not a single number. A rank-one global
latent is absorbed by the chain's fitted $\corr$ and never reverses the ordering in the
swept range: the ratio falls only from $\misRatioGlobalEzeropzeroD$ to
$\misRatioGlobalEonepzeroD$ across the swept range, up to $\beta=\misEpsMaxGlobal$. An AR(2) second coefficient
reverses it between $\misBreakLoArtwoD$ and $\misBreakHiArtwoD$ at both sizes, and a
site-dependent $\corr$ between $\misBreakLoSitevarD$ and $\misBreakHiSitevarD$ at
$\nseq=2048$. Long-range precision coupling does not reverse it anywhere we swept: the
ratio decays to $\misRatioLongrangeEzeropfourD$ at $\gamma=\misEpsMaxLongrange$, a tie rather than a
crossing. We report brackets and not fitted crossings, because with
$\misSeeds$ seeds the paired log-ratio is resolved to a few thousandths and the
crossing sits between grid points either way.

The two sizes separate the statistical gain of the structure at small $\nseq$ from its
asymptotic bias at large $\nseq$. At zero strength the EM--BP error falls from
$\misArtwoEmbpEzeropzeroC$ to $\misArtwoEmbpEzeropzeroD$ between $\nseq=512$ and
$2048$, which is estimation; at the largest AR(2) strength it moves from
$\misArtwoEmbpEzeropfourC$ to $\misArtwoEmbpEzeropfourD$, which is not. Past the
break-even the chain estimator has converged to a wrong law and more data no longer
buys anything back --- exactly the behaviour \cref{sec:theory} attributes to a model
whose bias, not whose variance, is binding.
