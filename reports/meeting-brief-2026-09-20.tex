\section{The question and the laboratory}

A diffusion model learns the score of a noised data law, $\nabla_x\log P_t(x)$, and for real data
nobody knows the target. We built a family where it is known exactly and is not trivial: a
first-order Markov chain of length $L$ with non-Gaussian innovations (Laplace, Student-$t$,
uniform, bimodal, a one-parameter family in between), noised coordinate by coordinate. Because
the noise attaches one factor per site, the posterior stays a chain and two sweeps of exact
message passing return the score. The covariance of every innovation family is the same by
construction, so the laboratory separates second-order structure (correlation $\rho$) from
higher-order structure (the shape of the innovation law) cleanly. \comp{Chapters 3, 6, 10}

Three questions were asked of it. What information survives the noise, order by order? Can the
dynamics be learned from noised sequences alone, and how efficiently? And what does an
estimator that knows the structure buy against a neural denoiser that does not, and where does
that stop?

\section{What stands, in one line each}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \hi{Noise is a low-pass filter on statistical order.} The Hermite moment of degree $m$ is
attenuated exactly by $e^{-mt}$; the Fisher information about a feature first visible at degree
$m$ decays as $e^{-2mt}$; the number of sequences needed for a fixed local precision grows as
$e^{2mt}$. Correlation lives at degree two, shape at degree four. Verified on the exact score,
with the constants. \comp{Chapters 2, 4, 9}

\item \hi{Second-order closure has an exact price.} Gaussian message closure computes the LMMSE of
the covariance twin (Proposition~1), and the gap to the true score is
$\tfrac16\|\kappa_4\|_F^2e^{-8t}$ to leading order. Adding the fourth-order term back closes
$\audEdgeClosedLaplaceTzeropsixeight$ of the gap at $t=\audEdgeLevelTzeropsixeight$ and all of it
from $t=2$. \comp{Chapters 9, 12}

\item \hi{The dynamics are learnable through the noise, and the information curve says where
learning stops.} From $\learnSize$ sequences the shape is recovered at the reference up to
$t=\learnLevelTzeropfour$; fits split at $t=\learnLevelTzeropseven$
($\audCollapsedTzeropseven$ of $\audCollapsedNTzeropseven$ collapse to a Gaussian innovation) and
collapse at $t=1$, one level below the point $t=\learnKurtBoundCross$ where the Cram\'er--Rao
reference crosses the coordinate's range. The correlation survives to the crossover.
\comp{Chapters 4, 14}

\item \hi{Capacity buys exactly the fourth-order structure.} A Gaussian-innovation kernel lands on
the LMMSE floor ($\capEmoneD$ against $\lmmseFloorLaplace$); an eight-component mixture reaches
$\capEmeightD$ at $M=2048$. Across the generalised-Gaussian family the gain is ordered by
$\kappa_4$ and reverses at the Gaussian member. The contrast holds in all $\regCells$
$(\rho,L)$ cells of a regime sweep, and in every cell the $C=1$ arm lands on the LMMSE floor to
$\regIdentMax$, which checks the whole apparatus. \comp{Chapters 12, 14, 16}

\item \hi{Against networks, the advantage is largest at equal information.} Under the standard
recipe (clean sequences, fresh corruption every step) the best of six architectures reaches
$\baseBestD$ at $M=2048$, a paired factor $\baseRatioBestD$ behind EM--BP. At EM--BP's own
interface (one noised view per sequence, no clean chain, no true score for model selection) the
best network reaches $\matchedBestD$, a paired factor $\matchedRatioBestD$
$[\matchedRatioBestDlo,\matchedRatioBestDhi]$; given the clean sequence as a target but still one
corruption, $\matchedFixedD$, a factor $\matchedFixedRatioD$. \comp{Chapters 7, 16, 18}

\item \hi{The advantage ends where the structure is wrong, at a strength we can compute.} Under an
AR(2) coefficient or a site-dependent $\rho$ the ordering reverses inside the swept range; a
rank-one global latent or a long-range coupling never reverses it. The large-sample error of the
misspecified EM--BP is a closed-form Kullback--Leibler projection bias $b(\varepsilon)$ that
reproduces the measured errors to within $\audBiasMaxAbsDevLagoneD$. \comp{Chapter 17}
\end{enumerate}

\section{What the audit changed}

These are corrections to what the 18 September report and the manuscript \emph{said}; no frozen
number was wrong.

\begin{itemize}
\item \hi{``Information-matched, $7.3\times$'' was the wrong arm.} That network saw the clean
chain as a regression target. The matched arm is $\matchedRatioBestD\times$; the $7.3\times$ arm
is now reported as ``even given the clean sequence''.
\item \hi{``Attains the Cram\'er--Rao bound at $1.02\times$'' is withdrawn.} The average mixed
levels where the fit is at the reference with levels where it is collapsed; the bound is for a
one-parameter family and the estimator is a mixture selected on validation. The paper now reports
the per-level picture of item~3 above.
\item \hi{``Locality is a ceiling'' is withdrawn.} The exact best radius-4 estimator has error
$\audLocFloorrfourRhozeropeightfive$ at $\rho=0.85$ against the window head's $\baseWindowD$: the
radius costs little, the architecture costs the rest.
\item \hi{Theorem~3 needs an exponential moment}, which its proof used and its statement lacked.
Restated; every family in the paper satisfies it except Student-$t$, whose heavy-tailed regime
is now a separate proposition. On a wide enough domain the sampled Student-$t_5$ chain reaches
the Hermite constant ($\audConstRatioStudentfiveTfour$ at $t=4$); its earlier ``departure'' was
grid truncation.
\item \hi{``All belief mass outside the domain'' was one or two sites in $8192$.} The
Student-$t$ row of the family ladder is now computed on a $(\phaseWideN,\phaseWideA)$ domain,
where it has stopped moving (by $\phaseStudentMoveWide$ between the two widest grids).
\item \hi{The departure of $\hat\rho$ from the reference at $t\ge1.4$ was partly budget.}
With the EM ladder extended to $\learnBudgetLadderMax$ iterations the error at $t=1.4$ falls
from $\learnRmseRhoTonepfour$ to $\learnBudgetRmseRhoTonepfour$; at $t=2$ from
$\learnRmseRhoTtwo$ to $\learnBudgetRmseRhoTtwo$ with the ladder still binding in
$\learnBudgetAtCapTtwo$ of cells. Past the crossover EM is as limited by its convergence as by
the information.
\item \hi{Model selection without the truth works.} Selecting EM--BP's iteration count on the
held-out one-view log-evidence instead of on validation risk against the true kernel gives
$\audSelEvidenceA$ against $\audSelOracleA$ at $M=32$ (the last rung: $\audSelLastA$).
\end{itemize}

\section{What is still running, and one thing that went wrong}

\begin{itemize}
\item \hi{E12b} (sample complexity by order, sixteen seeds, five levels) is two thirds through on
the cluster. Its analysis was amended before any data: the target is the exact information
curve, whose local slopes on the swept levels are $6$--$7$ for shape and $3.8$--$3.9$ for
correlation, not the asymptotic $8$ and $4$.
\item \hi{E14} (the matched comparison across three correlations and two chain lengths) is the
claim a referee will press on hardest. Its first runs were void: the driver re-created the
random generator inside its sampling loop, so every training chain was the same draw, and both
arms fitted $2048$ copies of one chain. Found by running two independent training functions on
the same data; fixed with an assertion; the corrected run is queued on the GPU partition and
takes about a day. Recorded as negative result N-22.
\item \hi{The quadrature envelope} now covers the longer chains: the worst drift on the
operating grid moves by a factor $\gridLenWorstRatioMax$ from $L=32$ to $L=128$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Decisions for the meeting}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \hi{Headline.} ``Structure beats networks at equal information by $\matchedRatioBestD\times$,
and the standard comparison understates it'' (recommended), or the standard-recipe factor of
$\baseRatioBestD\times$ with the matched one as a robustness check.
\item \hi{Efficiency.} Present learning through noise as ``at the reference while identifiable,
collapse where the reference says'' (recommended) rather than as a Cram\'er--Rao efficiency
statement.
\item \hi{Framing before or after E14.} Wait for the corrected E14 (about a day) before fixing
the regime sentence (recommended), or state the headline at one point and add the sweep as it
lands.
\item \hi{Page budget.} The ICML body is four pages over. The recorded cut order moves the
family figure, the generation table, the length table and the regime paragraph to the appendix;
the theory verification is compressed. Agreement needed on what must stay in the body.
\item \hi{Compendium.} A self-contained compendium (twenty chapters: from cumulants and Hermite
polynomials to belief propagation, quadrature, EM, quasi-likelihood, score matching and the
protocol) is being written so that every line of the paper can be checked without the
literature. Its scope and level are for you to set.
\end{enumerate}
